Hostel for NEET Students

NEET Study Environment

Jyoti Girls PG is purpose-built for NEET aspirants who need disciplined schedules, strong supervision, and the shortest commute to Osmanpura coaching classes.

Focused Study Infrastructure

Silent Library Floor

Ergonomic desks, pin-drop silence, and power backup ensure uninterrupted revision till midnight.

Smart Room Layouts

Rooms are organised with personal study tables, task lighting, and storage so each resident has a private prep corner.

Exam Mock Zone

Weekly mock tests are conducted in our seminar room with OMR sheets and invigilation to mimic centre conditions.

Academic Mentoring & Tracking

  • Subject mentors from Osmanpura institutes host doubt clinics twice a week.
  • Progress dashboards capture test scores, attendance, and focus areas for targeted improvement.
  • Dedicated quiet hours after dinner reserve time for last-mile revision.
  • Parents get fortnightly performance updates and behavioural reports.

Location Advantage for NEET Aspirants

Walk to Coaching

Resonance, Aakash, and local NEET academies around Osmanpura are within 5–10 minutes on foot, saving travel time.

Healthy Daily Routine

Early breakfast, curated snacks, and hydration stations keep energy levels steady during back-to-back classes.

Peer Accountability

Residents preparing for similar exams motivate each other, share resources, and maintain discipline.
